Selections and Masks

A selection is a recipe of steps rather than a channel of pixels. A rectangle, a Color Selection click, a brush stroke, or a refined edge each become a step, so a mask follows its layer when it moves, costs a few hundred bytes to save, and selects the same edge in the export as in the preview.
