Opening and Starting Out

ImageGui opens PNG, JPEG, WebP, TIFF, GIF, BMP, and ICO pictures, Photoshop files with their layers, and its own .imagegui projects. A project links to the original pictures rather than copying them, so it stays small, and a packaged copy carries them along when the project has to travel.
