Color Management and HDR

A picture can work in linear sRGB or in ACEScg, the working space film, visual effects, and game studios share, where light brighter than white keeps its value. An ACEScg picture is shown and exported through the ACES 2.0 output transform, so a bright sun rolls off rather than clipping, and OpenEXR files come in and go out without losing their range.
