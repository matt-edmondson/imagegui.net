Game Art and Pixel Art

Pixel art is drawn in whole pixels against a palette, animated with onion skin, and exported at whole multiples. Sprite sheets, trim sheets, 9-slice frames, indexed PNGs, TGA files, and normal maps come out in the forms Unity, Godot, Unreal, and the other engines read, and an export target rewrites them every time the project is saved.
