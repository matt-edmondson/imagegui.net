Layers

A document is a tree of image, paint, text, fill, shape, and placed-project layers in groups. Every layer change is one undo step, an opacity drag included, and blending happens in linear light, so the gray that leaves Overlay unchanged is half the light rather than an sRGB 50% swatch.
